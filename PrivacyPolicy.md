# Privacy Policy for QuickPersonalFinance

**Last Updated: October 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **QuickPersonalFinance** ("we," "us," or "our") handles data when you use our mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the terms outlined below.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Usage

#### User-Entered Financial Data
* **Manual Input Only:** All financial logs, budgets, income, and expenses are entered manually by you.
* **No Server Storage:** This financial data is processed and stored strictly on your local device. We do not transmit, view, or store your personal financial data on external servers.

#### Automatically Collected Diagnostic Data
To ensure stability and fix bugs, our app uses standard third-party developer tools provided by Google (Firebase and Crashlytics). These tools collect:
* **Crash Logs:** Technical stack traces and details about what caused a crash.
* **Device Metrics:** System configuration data, device model, and operating system version.
* **Identifiers:** Randomly generated, anonymous installation identifiers used solely to count active installations and aggregate crash trends.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services

We integrate the following third-party software development kits (SDKs) to maintain app performance:
* **Google Firebase & Crashlytics:** Used strictly for performance monitoring, diagnostics, and bug tracking. No personally identifiable financial data is shared with these services. Please review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how they process diagnostic data.

---

### 3. Data Protection and Security

* **Local Storage:** Because your financial data remains on your physical mobile device, the security of that data relies heavily on your device's native security settings (PIN, password, biometrics).
* **Encryption:** We leverage standard mobile platform security protocols to safeguard local data storage.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended to attract or target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document within the app package or app store listing.
